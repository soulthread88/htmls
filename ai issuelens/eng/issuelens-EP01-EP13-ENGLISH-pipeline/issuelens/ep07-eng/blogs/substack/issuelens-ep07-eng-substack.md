## 🛡️ Action Items

**Audit Your Apps**
Review the privacy policies of every Chinese-origin app on your devices. Revoke permissions for location, microphone, and contacts unless strictly necessary. Consider alternatives for sensitive communications.

**Know Your Cloud**
Ask your company's IT department to disclose the country of ownership and data storage location of all cloud services used. If any are Chinese-owned, demand a risk assessment and transition plan.

**Ask Your Carrier**
Contact your mobile carrier and ask what 5G equipment manufacturers power your network. If Huawei or ZTE equipment is still in use, file a formal inquiry — and support full CHIPS/FCC funding to complete the replacement program.

**Engage Congress**
Contact your representatives to support data localization legislation, the No Foreign AI Applications Act, full FCC rip-and-replace reimbursement, and a federal framework restricting Chinese cloud access to critical U.S. infrastructure.

**Share This**
Tech sovereignty is not a government problem — it's a citizen awareness problem. Share this with your network, your business partners, and your community. An informed public is the first line of defense.
