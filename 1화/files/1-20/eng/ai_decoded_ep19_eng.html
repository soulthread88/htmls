⚡ 3 Major AI Voice Assistants — Compared & Explained
💡 Did You Know?
Over 100 million Americans have already tried an AI tool — and the easiest place to start is right here, right now. Let's go!
1 📱 Google Assistant (Android) — Say "OK Google"
Built into Android smartphones. Say "OK Google" or press the home button to activate. Excellent at Korean and English understanding, and great for controlling Google apps — Maps, YouTube, Gmail. Go to Settings → Google → Google Assistant to turn it on.
2 🍎 Siri (iPhone) — Say "Hey Siri"
Built into all iPhones. Say "Hey Siri" to activate. Go to Settings → Siri & Search to turn it on. Perfect for Apple apps — FaceTime, Calendar, Reminders. iOS 18 now includes a ChatGPT connection for even smarter answers!
3 🔊 Amazon Alexa — Say "Alexa"
Primarily used on Amazon Echo smart speakers. Just say "Alexa" and it responds instantly — no button needed! Great for music, smart home control, shopping lists, and general questions. Many seniors love it for its always-on simplicity.

Voice Command Cheat Sheet — Try These Today! Practical AI for your real, everyday American life
Jake: "Memorize these commands and you'll never fumble for a button again! Practice out loud right now!"
1. Phone & Text Commands
"Call Jake" / "Text my daughter: Did you eat dinner?" / "Check my missed calls" — just say the name and your voice assistant finds the contact and connects you instantly.
2. Alarms & Reminders
"Set an alarm for 7am tomorrow" / "Remind me to take my medication at 1pm" / "What's on my schedule today?" — no more searching through apps with tiny icons!
3. Music & YouTube
"Play some country music on YouTube" / "Turn the volume down" / "Pause the music" — enjoy music even when your eyes are tired or your hands aren't cooperating.
4. Weather & Daily Info
"What's today's weather?" / "What time is it?" / "How far is it from New York to Boston?" / "Where's the nearest pharmacy?" — instant answers to everyday questions.

🔍 Breaking Down Today's Saying
"Actions speak louder than words."
actions (noun/verb/adj) Things you actually do — behaviors, not just intentions or promises
speak (noun/verb/adj) To communicate — here it means "to express" or "to show"
louder (noun/verb/adj) More powerful, more noticeable — comparative form of "loud"
words (noun/verb/adj) What you say, promise, or plan — as opposed to what you actually do
📌 Word-for-word meaning:
Literally: "The things you do communicate more powerfully than the things you say." Promises mean nothing if you don't follow through.
📌 In plain English:
Talk is cheap. What you actually do — your real behavior — shows who you truly are. Anyone can promise anything; not everyone delivers.
📌 Real-life analogy:
Imagine Grandpa Bob saying "I'll learn to use voice assistants someday" — but never actually trying. Compare that to Grandpa saying "Hey Siri!" and actually using it today. That second Grandpa? His actions speak louder than words!

American English Word Power Essential AI vocabulary every senior should know!
🇺🇸 AI Vocabulary — Top 3 This Episode
1. Activate (verb)
To turn something on or make it start working. "Say 'Hey Siri' to activate your iPhone's voice assistant instantly."
2. Command (noun)
An instruction or order you give to a device or person. "'Set an alarm for 7am' is a voice command that Siri and Google Assistant both understand."
3. Hands-free (adjective)
Operating without needing to use your hands — controlled by voice alone. "Voice assistants make your phone completely hands-free — perfect for seniors with vision or mobility challenges!"
