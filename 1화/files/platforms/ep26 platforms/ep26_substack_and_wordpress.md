### 🌙 What Your Smartwatch Tracks While You Sleep

Every night, your brain cycles through four stages of sleep:

**Deep Sleep** — When your body physically repairs itself. Muscles heal. Immune system strengthens. This is the most valuable sleep of all — and the one seniors most commonly lose with age.

**REM Sleep (Rapid Eye Movement)** — When you dream. When memories consolidate. When your emotional regulation resets for the next day. Short REM sleep leads to brain fog, irritability, and difficulty concentrating.

**Light Sleep** — The gateway between waking and deep sleep. You spend more time here than anywhere else.

**Awakenings** — The moments you actually wake up. Your Galaxy Watch logs every single one.

From all of this, the app calculates your **Sleep Score** — a number from 0 to 100 that tells you, at a glance, how restorative your sleep was last night.

---

### 📱 How to Find Your Sleep Score (Step by Step)

**Samsung Galaxy Watch:**
1. Open Samsung Health on your smartphone
2. Tap the "Sleep" card
3. See your score + the color graph below
   - Dark blue = deep sleep
   - Light blue = light sleep
   - Purple = REM
   - White gaps = times you woke up

**Apple Watch:**
1. Open Health app → Browse → Sleep
2. Look for your deep sleep duration
3. Check your weekly trend — is it improving or declining?

**No smartwatch? Use Sleep Cycle (free app):**
Place your phone face-down on your nightstand. The app uses your microphone to analyze your breathing sounds and determine your sleep stage throughout the night. It also wakes you at your lightest sleep phase — so you actually feel rested.

---

### 📊 What the Numbers Mean

| Score | What It Means |
|---|---|
| 70–100 | ✅ Good quality sleep |
| 50–69 | 🟡 Fair — room for improvement |
| 0–49 | 🔴 Poor — something is disrupting your sleep |

If you're consistently under 50, your body is struggling. This is worth a conversation with your doctor — especially if you bring the actual data.

**"I'm tired" is vague.**
**"My deep sleep averaged 22 minutes this past week — here's the graph from Samsung Health" — that's a productive medical conversation.**

### 🚨 A Note on Sleep Apnea

Galaxy Watch Ultra and Apple Watch Series 10 and later now detect breathing irregularities during sleep. If your watch sends you a breathing disturbance alert, please take it seriously and schedule a follow-up with your physician.

*Important: Smartwatch sleep monitoring is not a medical diagnostic device. A formal sleep apnea diagnosis requires a physician-ordered sleep study. The watch provides signals, not diagnoses.*

*Source: "Wearable Artificial Intelligence for Sleep Disorders: Scoping Review," Journal of Medical Internet Research, May 2025*

### 💡 Three Habits — Starting Tonight

Grandpa Bob's sleep score went from **42 to 67 in one week** using only these three changes:

**1. Phone off at 9 PM**
Blue light from screens suppresses melatonin production. One hour of screen-free time before bed makes a measurable difference.

**2. No coffee after 2 PM**
Caffeine's half-life is 5 to 7 hours. Your 3 PM coffee is still 50% active in your bloodstream at 9 PM. Swap to herbal tea.

**3. Same bedtime every night — including weekends**
Your circadian rhythm is a biological clock that runs on consistency. Irregular sleep times are one of the leading disruptors of sleep quality in seniors.
