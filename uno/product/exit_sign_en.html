TUNNEL EVACUATION GUIDANCE

// DISTANCE-MARKING EXIT SIGN

Waterproof & Dustproof
Distance-Marking Exit Sign

UNO SYSTEM|EL-D · EL-D1

An exit sign mounted on the tunnel wall that displays the remaining distance to the portal or the nearest evacuation cross-passage so occupants can evacuate quickly in a fire. A continuously trickle-charged emergency battery is built in, keeping the sign effectively lit for more than 60 minutes during a power outage, and Edge-Light high-brightness LEDs coupled to a light guide plate deliver uniform luminance. The hinged stainless enclosure is easy to mount and open, and is rated IP 66/67 for dust and water protection.

// Features

Key Features

💡
Edge-Light Guide Plate Design
Light is injected into the edge of a light guide plate, allowing a slim profile. Because the light spreads evenly across the entire display face, brightness is more uniform and luminance is higher than in conventional exit signs. The display face delivers 450 cd/m² or more, and 200 cd/m² or more even in emergency mode.
🖼
Image / Video
🔆
High-Brightness LED Chip Light Source
High-brightness LED chips serve as the light source. Compared with conventional fluorescent or CCFL lamps, the source lasts far longer and draws less power, making it economical over a long service life. It operates at 40 W in day mode and 20 W in night mode (AC 220 V, ±5%).
🖼
Image / Video
🛡️
IP 66/67 Dustproof & Waterproof Construction
IP 66/67 dust and water protection allows reliable use in dusty, humid tunnel environments. The enclosure is rated IP67, shielding the internal light engine and circuitry from dust ingress and immersion.
🖼
Image / Video
🔋
Built-In Emergency Battery
A continuously trickle-charged emergency battery (Ni-MH 12 V) is built in. Even during a power outage the sign stays effectively lit for more than 60 minutes, so evacuation guidance is never interrupted when mains power is lost.
🖼
Image / Video
🧭
Distance to Portals and Cross-Passages
Mounted on the tunnel wall, the sign shows left and right directional arrows together with the numeric distance to the portal or the evacuation cross-passage. Even amid smoke and congestion, occupants can immediately judge the nearest escape route and how far it is.
🖼
Image / Video
🔧
Hinged SUS 304 Enclosure
The enclosure is made of 1.5 mm stainless steel (SUS 304), giving it strong resistance to corrosion and impact. Its hinged construction makes mounting and opening easy, simplifying installation and maintenance, and it is optimized for tunnel wall mounting.

// System Configuration

System Configuration

STEP 01
🔌
Normal Operation — Continuous Lighting and Battery Charging
In normal operation the display face is lit from the AC 220 V mains supply while the built-in emergency battery is trickle-charged continuously. Separate day and night lighting modes vary power draw, cutting energy costs over long-term operation.
  • Mains AC 220 V lighting (day 40 W / night 20 W)
  • Continuous trickle charging of the emergency battery (Ni-MH 12 V)
  • Display luminance held at 450 cd/m² or more
  • High-brightness LED chips — long life, low power

STEP 02
🔥
Fire or Outage — Automatic Switchover to Backup Power
If a fire cuts the mains supply, the sign switches automatically to the built-in battery and begins emergency lighting. It holds 200 cd/m² or more even in emergency mode and stays effectively lit for more than 60 minutes, maintaining guidance until evacuation is complete.
  • Outage detected → automatic switchover to battery backup
  • Emergency-mode luminance of 200 cd/m² or more
  • Effective operation for 60+ minutes (Ni-MH 12 V / 60 min)
  • IP 66/67 construction operates through smoke and humidity
  • Can interlock with tunnel safety systems (FACP, emergency broadcasting)

STEP 03
🚶
Evacuation Guidance — Direction and Distance at Once
The left and right arrows and the distance figures light simultaneously, so occupants can instantly see the direction of the nearest portal or cross-passage and how far it remains. Installed at regular intervals along the tunnel wall, the signs guide the evacuation route continuously over the entire length.
  • Left/right arrows plus distance in meters, shown together
  • Distance guidance to portals and evacuation cross-passages
  • Installed at regular intervals along the tunnel wall for a continuous escape route
  • Uniform surface emission for long-range visibility
