Metro Tunnel Fire Detection

// Fiber-Optic Linear Heat Detection · DTS

Metro Tunnels
Fiber-Optic DTS Fire Detection

UNO SYSTEM|Bandweaver FireLaser DTS

A metro tunnel is a long, enclosed space in which a train fire or overheating cables and equipment directly threaten passenger evacuation. Bandweaver FireLaser DTS installs the sensing fibre continuously along the tunnel ceiling and side walls, monitoring kilometres of tunnel to the metre and passing the ignition point straight to the supervisory and smoke-control systems. As at Marina Bay, it is metro-optimised LHD for large underground interchange sections.

// Features

Key Features

🌡️
Distributed Temperature Sensing (DTS)
Raman-based distributed temperature sensing turns the whole fibre into a single continuous sensor. Heating that begins anywhere in a long tunnel is caught at once, and combined fixed-temperature, differential and rate-of-rise logic keeps false alarms to a minimum.
🖼
Image / Video
📍
Metre-Level Location
When local overheating or fire is detected, the exact section of the sensing line is computed as a metre-level coordinate. Even across kilometres of metro tunnel the precise ignition point appears on the control screen, cutting initial response time dramatically.
🖼
Image / Video
⚡
Intrinsically Safe, Unpowered Sensing Line
The sensing fibre carries no electricity and is a purely passive element. It is unaffected by electromagnetic interference and cannot spark, so it can be installed directly in explosion-hazard and high-voltage areas.
🖼
Image / Video
🛡️
Durability in Harsh Environments
Protected by metal armour and a flame-retardant jacket, the sensing cable operates dependably for years amid dust, moisture, corrosion and vibration. Its construction suits sites where maintenance is difficult, giving a long service life without re-installation.
🖼
Image / Video
🔗
Intelligent FACP Interlock
The DTS controller integrates with the Intelligent FACP and the SCADA supervisory system over standard protocols. Per-zone alarms and interlocked equipment control are automated, and heating history accumulates as trend data.
🖼
Image / Video
📈
Predictive-Maintenance Trending
Temperature change is logged continuously for every section, identifying abnormal heating and overloaded sections in advance. Warning comes before a fire can develop, shifting safety management from reactive response to predictive maintenance.
🖼
Image / Video

// System Configuration

System Configuration

STEP 01
🧵
Fibre Sensing Line Installation
The fibre sensing cable is installed continuously along the route to be monitored, reusing existing structures to keep construction work to a minimum.
  • Installed directly along the target run
  • Up to 8 km on a single channel
  • Armoured, flame-retardant, intrinsically safe sensing line
  • Existing routes can be reused

STEP 02
🖥️
FireLaser DTS Controller Analysis
The FireLaser DTS controller analyses the Raman scatter signal, builds a temperature profile of the whole run and maps hot spots in real time.
  • Temperature derived by Raman OTDR
  • Fixed-temperature, differential and rate-of-rise decisions per section
  • Hot spots mapped to the metre
  • Simultaneous multi-channel monitoring
  • Temperature history logged

Supervisory Interlock & Alarm
Results are sent to the Intelligent FACP and SCADA supervisory system, raising per-zone alarms and driving interlocked control.
  • Standard interlock with the Intelligent FACP
  • Live location shown on the control screen
  • Automatic per-zone alarms and equipment interlock
  • Remote web monitoring
