⚡ 3 Steps to Find the Lowest Price with AI
💡 Did You Know?
Over 100 million Americans have already tried an AI tool — and the easiest place to start is right here, right now. Let's go!
1 Google Shopping — Compare All Stores at Once
Go to Google and type your product name + 'price comparison.' Click the Shopping tab. Google instantly shows you the same product from multiple sellers with prices, shipping costs, and delivery times all in one view. Sort by 'Price: Low to High' to find the best deal!
2 CamelCamelCamel — Track Price History
Visit camelcamelcamel.com and search for any Amazon product. You'll see a full price history graph showing the highest, lowest, and average price over time. This tells you if today's price is truly a deal — or if you should wait for a sale!
3 ChatGPT — Ask for Smart Product Recommendations
Type: 'Recommend the best blood pressure monitor under $70 for a 70-year-old with arthritis.' ChatGPT analyzes features, ease of use, and reliability — not just price. It's like having a knowledgeable friend who has done all the research for you!

Saying Deep Dive Understand every word — and connect it to AI!
🔍 Breaking Down Today's Saying
"Look before you leap."
Look (noun/verb/adj) (verb) to examine carefully; to gather information and observe before acting
Before (noun/verb/adj) (prep/conj) earlier in time; prior to a specific action or event occurring
Leap (noun/verb/adj) (verb) to jump; here used figuratively to mean making a hasty decision or big commitment
Think (noun/verb/adj) (verb) to consider carefully; to use your mind and judgment before taking action
📌 Word-for-word meaning:
"Look" (examine the situation carefully) "before" (prior to the moment of action) "you" (the person about to act) "leap" (make a big jump or sudden commitment).
📌 In plain English:
Don't rush into decisions. Take a moment to gather information, think it through, and examine your options before you commit. This applies to jumping over a ditch — or clicking the Buy Now button online!
📌 Real-life analogy:
Imagine you're at the edge of a ditch. If you just jump without looking, you might land in mud or hurt yourself. But if you take two seconds to look — check the width, find the best spot — you land safely on the other side. Smart shopping works exactly the same way.
🤖 Connect It to AI!
AI shopping tools are built for people who look before they leap. Google Shopping, CamelCamelCamel, and ChatGPT together give you a complete look at prices, history, and product quality — before you commit a single dollar. Never leap blind again!
"Look before you leap." — this saying perfectly describes how AI works! 🌟
🧠 Memory Tip! Remember: LOOK first, LEAP second — always in that order! Example: 'My neighbor paid $90 for the same blood pressure monitor I bought for $62 on Amazon. That's what happens when you leap without looking! I always use Google Shopping first now.'

🇺🇸 AI Vocabulary — Top 3 This Episode
1. Price Comparison
Checking the same product across multiple stores to find the lowest total price (including shipping). AI tools like Google Shopping do this across thousands of stores in under one second.
2. Price History
A record of how a product's price has changed over time. CamelCamelCamel shows you whether today's 'sale' price is genuinely a discount — or just the normal everyday price with a fake sale tag.
3. Customer Reviews
Feedback and star ratings from people who already bought the product. AI now reads and summarizes hundreds of reviews in seconds — showing you the key Pros and Cons so you don't have to read them all yourself.
