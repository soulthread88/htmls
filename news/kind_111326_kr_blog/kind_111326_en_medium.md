## 1. Jensen Huang steps down at Nvidia
Nvidia's board said on Nov. 12 (US time) that Huang resigned effective immediately; Harvey Jones becomes Nvidia's first board chair. Shares fell roughly 13% intraday. Nvidia is the largest buyer of high-bandwidth memory (HBM) from Samsung and SK hynix.
- **Optimists:** a shift to board-led governance; earnings stay on schedule.
- **Skeptics:** a sudden leadership gap could delay AI spending and hit Korean chip exports.
*Sources: MTN (Kim Ju-young), iNews24 (Park Ji-eun)*

## 2. Lee's "multilateral super week"
National Security Adviser Wi Sung-lac announced the trip; APEC agenda items include AI cooperation, demographic change and Middle East energy shocks. *Source: Korea.kr policy briefing*

## 3. Ten on-device AI chips by 2030
The trade ministry finalized a roadmap for chips for autonomous cars, home appliances, robots and drones. *Source: Korea.kr*

## 4. Kim Jong Un inspects an east-coast naval base
State media reported Kim ordered completion "within the period set by the Party." *Sources: Newsis, Yonhap*

## 5. Son Heung-min and the "1996 line" issue joint statement
Five national-team players apologized for concern over discord reports and vowed to "prove it with performance." Korea plays Saudi Arabia (Nov. 13) and Qatar (Nov. 17). *Source: Wikitree*
