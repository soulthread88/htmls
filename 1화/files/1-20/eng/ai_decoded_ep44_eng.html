Over 200 million Americans have photos sleeping on their phones that nobody ever sees. AI video tools like Canva AI and CapCut can wake them up — for FREE.
1
📱 Round Up Your Photos (Pick 10–20)
Open your phone's photo gallery and choose 10 to 20 of your favorite pictures around one theme — grandkids' birthdays, last summer's trip, a holiday dinner. The more consistent the theme, the more beautiful the final video. You can't do this wrong! 📷

🎞️ Upload to an AI App (Canva or CapCut — Both Free!)

3
🎵 Pick Your Music & Hit Generate!
Choose from the AI's music suggestions — or add your own favorite song. Hit the "Generate" button and in just 1–2 minutes your family movie is ready! Share it immediately by text message, email, or social media. Guaranteed tears of joy. 🥹

🇺🇸 This Episode's AI Vocabulary — Top 3
1. GENERATE
(verb) — In AI language, "generate" means the AI creates something new from your input. When you press "Generate Video," the AI is building your movie from scratch based on the photos and music you gave it.
Example: "I uploaded 15 photos and hit Generate — the AI made a two-minute family film in 90 seconds!"
2. TRANSITION
(noun) — The smooth visual effect between two photos in your video. Think of it like turning a page — instead of a hard cut, AI adds a gentle fade, slide, or swipe. It's what makes AI videos look professionally made.
Example: "The transitions in my video were so beautiful — each photo just melted into the next!"
3. TEMPLATE
(noun) — A ready-made design layout that AI uses to give your video a specific style and feel. Canva and CapCut both offer hundreds of free templates — birthday, travel, romantic, seasonal. You just pick one and the AI fills it with your photos!
Example: "I used the 'Autumn Memories' template and it made my fall trip look like a National Geographic special!"
