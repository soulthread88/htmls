🔍 Breaking Down Today's Saying
"A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step"
Journey (noun/verb/adj) A long process or adventure — not just physical travel, but any great undertaking in life
Thousand (noun/verb/adj) A large, poetic number — representing any big, ambitious, seemingly overwhelming goal
Begins (noun/verb/adj) Starts — the very first action that sets everything else in motion
Single Step (noun/verb/adj) One small, humble action — the reminder that even the tiniest move counts and creates momentum
📌 Word-for-word meaning:
Word for word: "A trip of one thousand miles starts with one step." Every great journey — no matter how enormous — has a starting point of just one small action taken at one moment in time.
📌 In plain English:
No matter how big and scary something looks, you only need ONE first step. You don't have to figure everything out at once. You don't have to be perfect. Just begin — and the rest naturally follows.
📌 Real-life analogy:
Think about when you learned to drive back in the day. The first moment in the driver's seat felt completely overwhelming — mirrors, pedals, traffic rules! But you started by just turning the key. One step. Then another. Then one day you were driving across the country without even thinking about it. Learning AI is exactly the same!

🇺🇸 AI Vocabulary — Top 3 This Episode
1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)
"Artificial" means man-made. "Intelligence" means the ability to think and learn. Put them together: AI = a man-made system that can think and learn like a human. Simple as that!
2. Algorithm
An "algorithm" is just a set of instructions — like a recipe. Your pancake recipe is an algorithm for making breakfast. AI uses algorithms to decide what to do next. No scary math required!
3. Machine Learning
Instead of being told every rule, an AI "learns" by seeing millions of examples — like how you learned to pick a good watermelon by pressing and tapping hundreds of them at the grocery store over the years!
