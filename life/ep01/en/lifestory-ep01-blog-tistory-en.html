2. Plot in Five Acts

Act 1: The list

Diane leaves Tom standing and hands over a list of prices.

Act 2: The pressure

Dropped spoons, a split check for a dinner Tom never ate, rumors around town, a mocked workshop, his name shrunk on the invitations. Tom nearly sells his workshop and his late wife's ring.

Act 3: The breaking point

Two weeks before the wedding, Diane pushes a mortgage on Grace's building, then texts: “Who does a carpenter think he is?”

Act 4: The payback

Richard reviews the evidence at a diner, then seats Tom himself: “Put that away, Diane.”

Act 5: Justice

Pruitt is arrested at the airport, Diane's friend is charged, and Diane's lake house and jewelry pay her debt.

3. Best Lines

“Tom, those are good hands. Honest hands.”
“Please walk Grace down the aisle. In that suit.”

4. Ending and Epilogue

Tom walks Grace down the aisle in his old suit. The favors are two hundred walnut spoons. A year later, Diane admits food tastes warmer eaten with that spoon.
