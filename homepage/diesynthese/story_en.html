Why “Die Synthese”?

Die Synthese is German for “synthesis.” The name is the message: Yemen, Seoul and beyond.

More than a cup

A space for discovering culture.

Die Synthese brings together Yemeni coffee, its origins, its people, its craft and today’s Seoul, creating a space where a cup of coffee opens the door to culture. Run as a partnership between Koreans and Yemenis, we serve nano-lot coffee sourced directly from the Haraz and Ibb regions.

Itaewon

Itaewon — our doorway, and the road beyond it.

Itaewon has always been Seoul’s meeting place — a neighborhood built by people who came from somewhere else. Die Synthese sits inside that history, not beside it. We don’t translate Yemen for a Seoul audience. We simply open the door and let the coffee speak for itself.

A history of the coffee trade

Through Yemen, to the world.

Yemen has played a major role in the history of coffee growing and trade through the port of Mokha. With every single-origin lot we import directly, Die Synthese hopes to carry that history forward.
