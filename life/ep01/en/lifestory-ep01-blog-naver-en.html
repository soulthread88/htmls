[Story Time] She Left My Dad Standing and Demanded $50K. Then Her Husband Grabbed a Chair…

Grab a coffee, friends. This one starts frustrating and ends SO satisfying. ☕

The dinner

A fancy country club.

The future mother-in-law slides over a typed list. $50,000. Flowers. A fur coat. Just numbers.

And the bride's dad, a carpenter, is standing. Nobody offered him a chair.

The spoons

He brought hand-carved walnut spoons with the couple's names on them.

“Love is measured in dollars, dear.”

The spoon hit the floor. He knelt to pick it up. Ouch. 😢

Plot twist: she already knew

The daughter was living that night for the second time. Last time, she lost her bakery and her dad. This time? She hit record. 👏

The payback

Family dinner. Same list. Still no chair. Then the quiet father-in-law walks in carrying a chair himself.

“Put that away, Diane. Before you embarrass yourself any further.”

You have to hear what happens next. 🥤
