🛡️ Action Items — What You Can Do Right Now

▶ Know What You're Playing
Check whether your favorite Korean-developed games — PUBG, Stellar Blade, Nikke, Seven Knights — are made by companies with Tencent as a major shareholder. Informed consumers shape market outcomes. Korea's DART filings are publicly accessible in English summary via global financial databases.

▶ Push for CFIUS Expansion
Advocate for legislation extending CFIUS-style review authority to cover Chinese investment in allied nations' strategic companies — particularly where those companies have substantial U.S. user bases or are integrated into U.S. defense supply chains. Several bipartisan bills have floated this concept in Congress.

▶ Support Allied Data Governance
Support U.S.-Korea diplomatic frameworks that include data localization, allied investment screening reciprocity, and joint intelligence-sharing standards on economic coercion. The bilateral security relationship should extend beyond military hardware to digital and economic sovereignty.

▶ Support Independent Ecosystems
Consciously support Korean gaming studios, entertainment companies, and fintech platforms that are not under Chinese capital influence. Consumer spending is the most direct market signal available. A healthy, independent Korean tech ecosystem is an American strategic interest.
