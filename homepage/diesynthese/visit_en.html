Nearby sights

Worth seeing in Itaewon.

Places you can explore while you’re in Itaewon. Please check each site for travel times, admission and opening information.

Source: Korea Tourism Organization

Landmark

Seoul Central Mosque

A religious and cultural space in the Itaewon area. Please check visiting hours, etiquette and which areas are open to visitors before you go.

Source: Korea Tourism Organization

Cafés & shopping street

Gyeongnidan-gil

A hillside street west of Itaewon Station lined with distinctive cafés, boutiques and galleries. A great place for a stroll after a cup of coffee.

Source: Korea Tourism Organization

Neighborhood

Haebangchon (HBC)

A quiet hillside neighborhood at the foot of Namsan, home to bookshops, bars and a wide mix of restaurants. One of the most walkable neighborhoods in Seoul.

Source: Korea Tourism Organization

Museum

War Memorial of Korea

A large national museum on Korea’s military history, a short walk from Samgakji Station. Please check exhibition and operating hours and admission requirements before you go.

Source: to be confirmed

Art museum

Leeum Museum of Art

A museum housing the Samsung family’s collection of traditional and contemporary Korean art, also known for its buildings designed by three different architects.

Source: to be confirmed

Walks & views

Namsan Park & N Seoul Tower

Follow the wooded trails up Namsan and you’ll be rewarded with sweeping views of N Seoul Tower and the city. You can go up on foot or by cable car.
