🛡️ Action Items — What You Can Do Right Now

▶ Choose American & Local First
When you buy from a local store, independent Amazon seller, or American brand — that money stays in your community as wages, taxes, and jobs. Even a small shift in spending habits, multiplied by millions of people, changes the economics. Price differences on Temu are often smaller than you think once you factor in quality and return friction.

▶ Support Small Business Saturday Every Day
The categories most devastated by Chinese platform dumping — home goods, apparel, toys, kitchenware — are exactly where your local small businesses operate. When a small business closes, it doesn't come back. The awareness gap is enormous: most American shoppers have no idea they're participating in market destruction.

▶ Share This — Knowledge Is the First Defense
Most Americans buying from Temu have no idea what de minimis is, what state subsidies do to American competitors, or what the Digital Silk Road is building inside U.S. infrastructure. Share this article with people in your life. The structural problem can only be fixed politically — and political will requires public awareness first.

▶ Contact Your Senators — De Minimis Reform Now
De minimis reform bills have stalled in Congress for years. The De Minimis Reciprocity Act and the FIGHTING for America Act both have bipartisan support but face fierce lobbying opposition. Contact your Senators directly and tell them: close the $800 loophole, require country-of-origin labeling on all de minimis packages, and protect American workers from state-subsidized dumping.
