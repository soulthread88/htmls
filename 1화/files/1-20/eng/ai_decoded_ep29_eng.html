1 Papago — Best for Asian Languages & Camera Translation
Papago (by Naver) excels at Korean, Japanese, and Chinese translation. Its standout feature: Camera Translation — point your phone at any foreign text and see the translation overlaid in real time. Perfect for menus, signs, and handwritten letters!
2 DeepL — Best for European Languages & Full Documents

Saying Deep Dive Understand every word — and connect it to AI!
🔍 Breaking Down Today's Saying
"It's not what you say, it's how you say it."
Say (noun/verb/adj) (verb) to speak words aloud; to express something in language; to communicate a message
How (noun/verb/adj) (adv/conj) in what way or manner; the method or style of doing something
Tone (noun/verb/adj) (noun) the overall quality, attitude, or feeling expressed in a voice or piece of writing
Express (noun/verb/adj) (verb) to convey a thought, feeling, or idea clearly through words, tone, or actions
📌 Word-for-word meaning:
"It's not what you say" = the actual words and content are not the most important part. "It's how you say it" = the tone, manner, expression, and feeling behind the words matter most.
📌 In plain English:
You can say 'I love you' in an angry tone and it sounds sarcastic. You can say 'good morning' warmly and it brightens someone's whole day. The same words land completely differently depending on HOW you say them.
📌 Real-life analogy:
Think of a gift wrapped in crumpled newspaper versus a gift wrapped in beautiful paper with a bow. The gift inside might be the same — but how it's presented changes everything. Language works exactly the same way.
🤖 Connect It to AI!
The best AI translators — like DeepL and ChatGPT — don't just translate words. They understand context, tone, and cultural nuance. They capture not just WHAT you say, but HOW you say it. That's what makes the difference between a translation that sounds robotic and one that sounds truly human.
"It's not what you say, it's how you say it." — this saying perfectly describes how AI works! 🌟
🧠 Memory Tip! Remember: WHAT vs HOW — the content vs the delivery. Example: 'My Japanese friend said the Papago translation sounded very natural — not stiff or robotic. That's because Papago understands that it's not just what you say, it's how you say it!'

🇺🇸 AI Vocabulary — Top 3 This Episode
1. Translation
Converting text or speech from one language into another. AI translation apps now do this instantly — in real time through your camera, microphone, or typed text — with remarkable accuracy.
2. Real-Time
Happening immediately as something occurs, without any delay. When Papago's camera translation works in real-time, you see the translation the instant you point your phone at foreign text.
3. Context
The surrounding words, situation, and cultural background that give language its full meaning. Great AI translators understand context — which is why DeepL and ChatGPT sound more natural than basic word-for-word translators.
