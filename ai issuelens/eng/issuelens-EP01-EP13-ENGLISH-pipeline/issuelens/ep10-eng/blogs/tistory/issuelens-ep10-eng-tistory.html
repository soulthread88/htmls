The ballot box is democracy's last line of defense. But what if the software tallying your votes, the hardware reading your ballot, and the telecom networks carrying election data have already been compromised — silently, from within the supply chain itself? This is not a hypothetical. U.S. intelligence agencies, Five Eyes allies, and the FBI have confirmed it: China is already inside. The war with no shots fired is targeting your vote.

01The Five Eyes Warning — Official Text & What It Means

🔍 Top Searches: "Five Eyes China election warning" · "China election interference Five Eyes" · "CISA China cyber threat election" · "Five Eyes advisory 2024 China"

The Five Eyes alliance — the intelligence partnership between the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand — issued an unprecedented joint warning about China's threat to democratic elections. The alerts specifically flag cyber penetration of election infrastructure and coordinated influence operations as the primary attack vectors.

🇺🇸 Official Source — CISA · FBI · ODNI Joint Statement (2024)
"The People's Republic of China (PRC) continues to pose the most broad, significant, and long-term counterintelligence and national security threat to the United States and our allies. PRC state-sponsored cyber actors are capable of directly threatening U.S. critical infrastructure, including election infrastructure, through their demonstrated capabilities to compromise networks and maintain persistence."
— CISA · FBI · ODNI Joint Statement (March 2024)
🇬🇧🇦🇺🇨🇦🇳🇿 Official Source — Five Eyes Joint Advisory on PRC Cyber Threats (2024)
"The PRC has the capability and intent to engage in sustained and broad-based foreign interference activities, including in the electoral processes of Five Eyes member states. This includes the use of cyber operations to conduct reconnaissance on election management bodies, political parties, and candidates, and to compromise election-related systems and data."
— Five Eyes Joint Cybersecurity Advisory (February 2024)
KEY FACT · CISA / FBI Official Assessment (2024)
40+ Nations Countries where China state-linked actors have conducted confirmed or suspected election-related cyber operations (ASPI estimate, 2024)

According to CISA, Chinese state-affiliated cyber groups — including Volt Typhoon and APT41 — have identified election management networks, voter registration databases, and political party internal communications as primary infiltration targets. — CISA Election Security: China Threats

02Volt Typhoon & Salt Typhoon — China's Two-Pronged Attack on U.S. Elections

🔍 Top Searches: "Volt Typhoon election infrastructure" · "Salt Typhoon telecom hack election" · "China hack US infrastructure 2025" · "FBI China election system compromise"

Following the Trump administration's confirmation in early 2025, the FBI, CISA, and NSA officially verified that Volt Typhoon has been silently pre-positioning malware inside U.S. critical infrastructure for years — including systems tied to election operations. Simultaneously, Salt Typhoon penetrated major U.S. telecom carriers to wiretap election officials and campaign staff during the 2024 presidential race.

🇺🇸 Official Source — FBI · CISA · NSA Joint Advisory on Volt Typhoon (2025)
"Volt Typhoon is not a financially motivated threat actor. They are a People's Republic of China state-sponsored group that has pre-positioned malware in U.S. critical infrastructure networks — including communications, energy, transportation, and water — for the purpose of launching destructive cyberattacks in the event of a major conflict or crisis. Their persistent access to these networks represents a direct and ongoing threat to national security, including the integrity of systems used in democratic processes."
— FBI · CISA · NSA Joint Advisory (2025)
🇺🇸 Official Source — CISA Advisory AA25-071A: Salt Typhoon Telecom Breach (2025)
"Salt Typhoon, a PRC-affiliated cyber espionage group, has compromised the networks of multiple U.S. and allied telecommunications providers. The campaign allowed the actors to steal call records, intercept private communications of government officials and political figures, and potentially access systems used in lawful intercept programs. Election officials and campaign staff were among those targeted."
— CISA Advisory AA25-071A (March 2025)
📌 VOLT TYPHOON vs. SALT TYPHOON — ROLES EXPLAINED

Volt Typhoon: A long-term pre-positioning operation embedded inside U.S. power grids, water systems, transportation networks, and communications infrastructure. CISA assesses this group as a "kill switch" — capable of triggering simultaneous infrastructure collapse during a geopolitical crisis, including during a U.S. election cycle to maximize social disruption.

Salt Typhoon: A signals intelligence operation that penetrated the networks of AT&T, Verizon, and other major U.S. carriers. The FBI officially confirmed that both major-party presidential campaign staff had their communications intercepted during the 2024 election. Election administrators and government officials were specifically targeted. — Washington Post (October 25, 2024)

03The Voting Machine Supply Chain — America's Hidden Vulnerability

🔍 Top Searches: "voting machine security China components" · "election equipment supply chain China" · "voting machine hack risk" · "CISA voting system security"

The cyber threat to U.S. elections goes far beyond hacking attempts. The deeper, more systemic problem is the supply chain of semiconductors, components, and software inside ballot-counting machines, electronic poll books, and election management systems. The critical question: Are Chinese-made components embedded in certified U.S. voting equipment — and if so, could they contain backdoors that no one has detected?

🇺🇸 Official Source — U.S. Senate Committee on Homeland Security Report (2024)
"Election infrastructure in the United States relies on a complex, often opaque supply chain involving components sourced from multiple countries, including the People's Republic of China. Certain components used in voting systems — including microcontrollers, memory chips, and network modules — have been identified as potentially manufactured by entities with ties to the PRC government or military. The Committee concludes that a comprehensive supply chain security audit of all federally certified election equipment is urgently needed."
— U.S. Senate Homeland Security Committee Report (2024)

04A-WEB — The Global Election Network & China's Influence Strategy

🔍 Top Searches: "A-WEB World Election Bodies" · "China influence global election organizations" · "election body international security" · "A-WEB election Korea China"

A-WEB (Association of World Election Bodies) is the world's largest international network of election management bodies, founded in 2013 with 119 member election commissions across the globe. Its secretariat is based in South Korea. A-WEB facilitates the sharing of election technology, expertise, and systems standardization between member countries. For the U.S., this network is both a diplomatic asset for democracy promotion and a potential concentration point for foreign intelligence targeting.

A-WEB — Key Facts (Official Website)
119 Nations Member election management bodies — the world's largest hub for electoral technology sharing and democratic governance standards

A-WEB's core mission is international sharing of election systems, technology, and institutional experience. The flip side: any actor capable of accessing the A-WEB network potentially gains insight into the election infrastructure architecture and technical vulnerabilities of 119 democracies simultaneously. — A-WEB Official Website

⚠️ Assessed Concern — Stimson Center Report (Labeled: Not Yet Officially Confirmed)

The Stimson Center's 2024 report assessed that "China is pursuing a strategy of leveraging international election bodies like A-WEB to cultivate influence over election management agencies in developing nations and steer them toward adopting election technology standards favorable to Beijing." The report further noted documented attempts by Chinese-affiliated entities to participate in technical advisory programs of international election bodies — though specific A-WEB involvement has not been officially confirmed by U.S. or Five Eyes agencies and should be treated as an assessed concern rather than verified fact.

— Source: Stimson Center, "China and Global Election Governance" (2024)
⚖️ BALANCED PERSPECTIVE

The opportunity: A-WEB gives democracies a formal multilateral channel to raise election security standards globally and to share best practices — exactly the kind of institution that can help insulate developing-world election bodies from Chinese influence, not expose them to it. U.S. engagement with A-WEB could serve as a counterweight to Chinese soft power in election governance.

The risk: A 119-country hub for election technology data is a high-value intelligence target. China's documented approach — targeting individual member nations directly rather than the institution itself — means the greatest vulnerability lies in member states with weak cybersecurity, not in A-WEB's own infrastructure. Strengthening individual members' defenses is the most urgent priority.

05🇺🇸 U.S. Election System Vulnerabilities — Confirmed Facts vs. Open Questions

🔍 Top Searches: "US voting machine security audit" · "election system hack confirmed" · "CISA election vulnerabilities 2024" · "US voter data breach China"

On U.S. election security, it is essential to separate officially confirmed facts from open concerns still under investigation. Both categories are serious. The distinction matters because conflating unverified claims with confirmed findings weakens public trust — which is itself a goal of Chinese information operations.

✅ CONFIRMED — CISA / FBI Official Finding
Compromised CISA confirmed PRC-linked actors successfully gained access to election-adjacent networks in multiple U.S. states during the 2020–2024 election cycles

CISA confirmed in its 2024 Election Security Report that Chinese state actors successfully probed and in some cases compromised networks adjacent to U.S. state election offices — including voter registration systems and county government IT infrastructure. No vote-count alteration has been confirmed, but the reconnaissance data obtained is assessed as a significant ongoing threat. — CISA Election Security Annual Report (2024)

06Global Election Interference Cases — Taiwan, Canada, UK, Australia, Germany

China's election interference is not limited to the United States. Five Eyes reports and international research institutions have compiled the following confirmed cases of Chinese-linked election interference operations.

Country / Year Confirmed Activity Source
🇨🇦 Canada (2021 & 2023) CSIS (Canadian Security Intelligence Service) officially confirmed Chinese election interference in federal elections. Chinese diplomats documented pressuring diaspora communities to work against specific candidates in public parliamentary testimony. CSIS Public Report (2023)
🇦🇺 Australia (2022) ASIO (Australian Security Intelligence Organisation) confirmed to parliament that a foreign government (China identified) provided funding and intelligence to support the defeat of specific candidates in the federal election. ASIO Annual Threat Assessment (2023)
🇹🇼 Taiwan (2024) China ran a large-scale disinformation and deepfake video campaign during Taiwan's presidential election. Meta removed thousands of China-linked accounts coordinating inauthentic behavior during the election period. Meta Adversarial Threat Report (2024)
🇬🇧 United Kingdom (2024) UK MI5 charged a China-linked individual for operating as a parliamentary lobbyist while collecting election intelligence. UK government officially confirmed APT31 hacked the Electoral Commission's servers, exposing data on ~40 million registered voters. UK NCSC Official Statement (2024)
🇩🇪 Germany (2021 & 2024) Germany's domestic intelligence agency (BfV) launched investigations into China-linked cyber operations targeting political parties and suspected political financing irregularities tied to Chinese entities. BfV Annual Report (2024)
🇬🇧 Official Source — UK NCSC Statement on China APT31 Electoral Commission Hack (2024)
"The UK government has today confirmed that the Chinese state-affiliated group known as APT31 was responsible for the cyberattack on the Electoral Commission's email and control systems between 2021 and 2022. While the attack had no impact on the way votes were cast, we believe that data relating to approximately 40 million people was accessible to the Chinese state. This is completely unacceptable. We will not tolerate attempts by any foreign actor to interfere with our democracy."
— UK NCSC Official Statement (March 25, 2024)

What Americans Can Do to Defend the Ballot Box

Citizens, journalists, Congress, and state officials each have a role in protecting democracy's last line of defense

ACTION 01
🔍
Demand a Supply Chain Audit
Contact your U.S. senators and representatives and demand they pass legislation requiring a full, independent supply chain security audit of every federally certified voting system — identifying every foreign-manufactured component and its origin.
ACTION 02
🖥️
Push for Open-Source Voting Code
Demand that election tabulation software be subject to independent security review by vetted, nonpartisan researchers. Germany and Finland use open-source or fully transparent election systems. The U.S. should too.
ACTION 03
🌐
Support Air-Gap Legislation
Congress must codify physical network separation between election management systems, voter registration databases, and public internet infrastructure. CISA has recommended this for years; it needs to be law, not a guideline.
ACTION 04
📰
Keep Facts & Fear Separate
China's information operations benefit when Americans can't tell confirmed threats from unverified claims. Always check sources. Share official CISA and FBI findings — not social media speculation. Informed panic is still panic.
ACTION 05
🤝
Support Five Eyes Coordination
Advocate for deeper U.S. election security intelligence sharing with Five Eyes allies and partner democracies. A threat to Canada's election is a threat to ours. Democratic solidarity — not isolation — is the answer to authoritarian interference.
