🛡️ Action Items — What You Can Do Right Now

▶ Delete TikTok — Or Seriously Limit It
Deleting TikTok does not delete your existing data from ByteDance's servers — you must separately submit a data deletion request through TikTok's privacy settings. For your children, remove the app entirely. Use Screen Time (iPhone) or Digital Wellbeing (Android) to set hard limits. If you keep it, never grant access to your contacts, microphone, or precise location.

▶ Talk to Your Kids — Really
The documented content asymmetry between TikTok and Douyin means your children are receiving a fundamentally different media diet than Chinese children of the same age. Have the conversation. Show them the data. Ask them what their algorithm is showing them. Digital literacy starts at home — and it's more urgent than any school curriculum.

▶ Audit Every Chinese App on Your Phone
TikTok is the most visible Chinese app — but WeChat, CapCut (ByteDance), Shein, Temu, AliExpress, and dozens of lesser-known apps operate under the same Chinese legal framework. Review every app's data permissions. Revoke access to your location, contacts, microphone, and camera for any app you don't actively trust.

▶ Demand a Genuine Structural Fix
Executive order extensions are not a solution. Contact your Senators and Representatives and demand one of two outcomes: a verified, complete divestiture of TikTok from ByteDance to a non-Chinese entity with binding data separation — or enforcement of the existing law. The RESTRICT Act, the SHIELD Act, and data broker legislation all need public pressure to advance.
