🚨 **Is your parent home alone right now? Read this.**

Every 11 seconds, an older American is rushed to the emergency room for a fall injury. Most of those falls happen at home. Most seniors are alone when it happens.

But did you know: **Apple Watch and Galaxy Watch can automatically call 911 when they detect a fall** — even if your parent is unconscious and can't reach the phone?

Here's how it works:
1. The watch detects the fall using its sensors
2. A 60-second countdown begins
3. If your parent doesn't respond — the watch calls 911 automatically
4. Your parent's GPS location is sent to emergency services
5. Your family receives an automatic text alert — immediately

**The setup takes 5 minutes. Here's exactly how:**

🍎 **Apple Watch:**
iPhone → Watch app → Emergency SOS → Fall Detection → ON
Then add Emergency Contacts (most families skip this — don't!)

📱 **Galaxy Watch:**
Samsung Health → Settings → Fall Detection → ON
Hold the Home button for 3 seconds = manual SOS

**The most important step most people miss:** Register emergency contacts. When 911 is called, your family gets an automatic text with your parent's exact GPS location. Register a family member, a neighbor, and a backup contact.

In our latest episode, Jake walks through every step — including what to do if a false alarm triggers and how to practice the cancel button with your parent.
