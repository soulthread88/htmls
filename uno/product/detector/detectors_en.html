// Fire Alarm Control Panel

DTS-Integrated
R-Type FACP

UNO SYSTEM|MDT-1000F / UFC-OR12

An R-type fire alarm control panel with built-in Distributed Temperature Sensing (DTS). Connected to a KFI-certified fiber-optic rate-of-rise distributed detector (DET24-74), it continuously measures the temperature profile along the entire fiber run, raises an alarm the moment an abnormal temperature is detected, and links in real time to fire department integrated monitoring facilities and integrated management systems.

// Features

Key Features

🌡️
Distributed Temperature Sensing (DTS)
A DTS system developed entirely with domestic technology is built in. Precision optics and signal processing deliver world-class accuracy and resolution.
🖼
Image / Video
💡
Fiber-Optic Distributed Detector
Connects to the KFI-certified fiber-optic detector (DET24-74). The optical fiber is housed in a stainless steel tube for fast thermal response and outstanding durability. Uses multimode GI 50/125 μm fiber.
🖼
Image / Video
⚡
Power Supply Surge Protection (6 kV/40 kA)
A 6 kV/40 kA surge protector is built into the power supply section to shield the circuitry from the strong surges and noise of underground utility tunnels. Optimized for long-term reliability.
🖼
Image / Video
🔋
Built-In Backup Power
Includes a Ni-Cd battery (DC 24 V/2,400 mAh) and a CR1220 lithium cell. Monitoring continues even when mains power is lost, and an LED lights immediately if the backup power fails.
🖼
Image / Video
📺
LCD Display and Intuitive Operation
The fire location and zone information appear instantly on the LCD. Main-bell stop, zone-bell stop, interlock stop, buzzer stop, reset, and auto-reset switches are laid out on the front panel for fast operation.
🖼
Image / Video
🏗️
Rugged Enclosure Design
SUS430 1.2T stainless steel enclosure. Dimensions 600 (W) × 750 (H) × 180 (D) mm, finished in Munsell No. 114 white. Operates reliably from −10 to 50 °C.
🖼

// System Configuration

System Configuration

STEP 01
🛰️
Continuous Monitoring in Normal Operation
The DTS launches a 1550 nm laser into the optical cable and analyzes the Raman backscatter to measure the temperature profile along the entire run continuously, on a cycle of 5 seconds to 30 minutes. The panel indicates normal AC power and normal backup power.
  • 1550 nm laser · Raman backscatter analysis
  • Continuous temperature profiling of the entire run
  • Measurement cycle of 5 seconds to 30 minutes
  • Normal AC and backup power indication

STEP 02
🔥
Fire / Abnormal Temperature Detection & Alarm
When a detector exceeds its set temperature, the fire location appears on the LCD, the fire LED lights, and the main and zone bells sound. The same alarm is raised when a manual call point is activated. If the optical cable breaks, the buzzer and the line-fault LED are activated.
  • LCD fire location display · fire LED
  • Main-bell and zone-bell alarms
  • Individual stop for main/zone bells, interlock, and buzzer
  • Line-fault monitoring (buzzer · fault LED · event logging)
  • Automatic switchover to backup power · auto reset

STEP 03
🖧
Integrated Management System Interlock
Real-time data and alarms are transmitted to the fire department's integrated monitoring facility and the control room's integrated management system through RS-485, RS-232, SCADA, and Ethernet interfaces. The panel adapts flexibly to a wide range of site conditions.
  • RS-485 / RS-232
  • SCADA / Ethernet
  • Interlock with the fire department's integrated monitoring facility
  • Interlock with the control room's integrated management system
