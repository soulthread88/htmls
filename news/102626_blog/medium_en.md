## 1. Kim Jongun visits Dandong and meets Wang Huning
**Facts.** On Oct 24, North Korean leader Kim Jongun made a day trip across the New Yalu River Bridge to Dandong, Liaoning Province. He met Wang Huning, chairman of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, laid flowers at a Korean War memorial, and toured a car plant.
**Context.** A rumor that Kim had died of a brain hemorrhage circulated the previous week; the visit shows it was false. The bridge, finished in 2014, officially opened on Oct 15.
**PRO:** Some analysts say wider North Korea–China channels can help manage regional tension.
**CON:** Others worry more trade and travel could weaken UN sanctions enforcement.
Source: [Segye Ilbo](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20261024506606)

## 2. Oh Sehoon acquitted on appeal
Seoul Mayor Oh Sehoon was found not guilty on appeal in a case over alleged third-party payment for opinion polls. Independent lawmaker Han Donghoon called him "someone to join forces with for a conservative win." The Democratic Party raised separate questions about the Han River bus project. A Supreme Court ruling may still follow.
Source: [News1](https://www.news1.kr/politics/assembly/6331003)

## 3. Typhoon Champi brings heavy rain to Jeju
Samgakbong on Mt. Halla recorded 295 mm of rain. On Oct 24, 25 flights were canceled and 218 delayed at Jeju. Travelers should check airline status before departure.
Source: [iNews24](https://inews24.com/view/2018680)

## 4. Crypto tax: another delay?
Korea's crypto-asset tax is scheduled for 2027. A new bill would push it to 2029. The presidential office said the matter is "under review, nothing decided."
**PRO:** Delay gives time to build reporting infrastructure and protect retail investors.
**CON:** Repeated delays undermine tax fairness with stock and other income.
Source: [Economist Korea](https://economist.co.kr/article/view/ecn202610250010)

## 5. Battery smoke on a Hong Kong–Jeju flight
On Oct 25, a power bank overheated and smoked aboard a Hong Kong–Jeju flight; one passenger suffered a finger burn. On Oct 11, a battery fire on a Cheongju–Jeju flight led to the evacuation of 168 people.
Source: [Newneek](https://newneek.co/@newneek/article/48051)

## 6. World: Lula wins a historic fourth term
In Brazil's Oct 25 runoff, President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, 81, won 51.79% to 48.21%, overturning a first-round deficit.
Source: [Kyunghyang Shinmun](https://www.khan.co.kr/article/202610262200005/)

## 7. On this day: Ahn Junggeun, Harbin, 1909
On Oct 26, 1909, Korean independence activist Ahn Junggeun shot Ito Hirobumi at Harbin Station.

## FAQ
**Is Kim Jongun dead?** No. He appeared publicly in Dandong on Oct 24.
**Is the "88% support retirement at 65" figure new?** No. It comes from a June 2026 survey by the Federation of Korean Trade Unions.
