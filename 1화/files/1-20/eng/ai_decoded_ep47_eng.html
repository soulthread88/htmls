⑤ AI Crash Course
3 Steps to Use AI for Your Retirement
Anyone can do this — no tech background needed
💡
Did You Know?
More than 100 million Americans have used an AI chatbot. The most popular ones — ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot — are completely free and available right now on your phone, tablet, or computer.
1
Step 1 — Tell AI Your Situation, Get Plain-English Analysis
Open ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot (all free!) and try this prompt:
"I'm a 70-year-old American. I get $1,200/month Social Security and $400/month pension. My monthly expenses are $1,800. Is my retirement income healthy? What should I watch out for?"
AI will explain the math, flag any gaps, and give you a clear starting picture — in plain English, no financial jargon.

⚠️ Never enter your Social Security Number, bank account numbers, or passwords into any AI tool.
2
Step 2 — Ask AI to Decode Financial Jargon You Don't Understand
Confused by your 401(k) statement? Medicare Advantage vs. Original Medicare? Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs)? Just ask AI:
"What is an RMD? Explain it like I'm 70 years old and didn't study finance."
AI will explain it in everyday American English, without the embarrassment of asking a banker who might rush you. No question is too basic.
3
Step 3 — Let AI Build Your "Smart Questions" List for Real Advisors
After chatting with AI, ask it:
"Based on my situation, what are the 5 most important questions I should ask my financial advisor next week?"
Print that list and walk into your bank or credit union ready. You'll get ten times more value from that 30-minute appointment. AI is your pre-game prep coach. 🏆

Understand Every Word — Connect It to AI!
Today's saying: "Don't wait until you're thirsty to dig the well."
🔍 BREAKING DOWN TODAY'S SAYING
"Don't wait until you're thirsty to dig the well."
WAIT verb To delay or hold off on taking action
THIRSTY adjective Needing water desperately; here: in urgent need
DIG verb To prepare, create, or build something with effort
WELL noun A water source; here: your safety net or financial backup
📌 WORD-FOR-WORD MEANING

Literally: Don't wait until you desperately need water before you start building the place to get water. By then, it's too late — you'll suffer while you dig.

📌 IN PLAIN AMERICAN ENGLISH

Don't wait for a financial crisis to start planning. Don't wait until you're sick to learn about Medicare. Don't wait until retirement to check your 401(k). Act early. Prepare now. Future-you will thank present-you.

Imagine a farmer who only thinks about water when his crops are dying in a drought. By then it's too late — the crops are gone. A smart farmer digs the well in spring, when there's time and energy to do it right. In retirement: spring is NOW, while you're healthy and stable. The drought is a financial emergency you can't afford.

⑪ American English Word Power
3 Essential AI + Finance Words This Episode
Know these — they'll come up everywhere
🇺🇸 AI VOCABULARY — TOP 3 THIS EPISODE
PROMPT
What it is: The question or instruction you type into an AI tool. A good prompt gives AI context and specifics. Example: instead of "help with money," say "I'm 71, receive $1,400/month Social Security, and spend about $1,900/month. What should I worry about?" That's a powerful prompt.
FDIC
What it is: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation — the U.S. government agency that protects your bank deposits up to $250,000 per person, per bank if the bank fails. Before you put savings anywhere, check: Is it FDIC-insured?
RMD
What it is: Required Minimum Distribution — the IRS requires you to withdraw a certain amount from your 401(k) or IRA each year after age 73. Miss it and you face a 25% tax penalty. Ask ChatGPT: "Explain RMDs in plain English for a 72-year-old."
