🛡️ Action Items — What You Can Do Right Now

▶ Enact a Korean NSI Act
Like the U.K.'s National Security and Investment Act, Korea urgently needs legislation mandating foreign investment screening in gaming, finance, platforms, and telecommunications.

▶ Clarify Values-Based Alliances
Deepen solidarity with the U.S., EU, and Japan on shared democratic values. Chinese retaliation is most devastating when faced alone — collective response is the answer.

▶ Diversify Exports & Sourcing
Like Australia, Korea must reduce China dependency and open markets in India, Southeast Asia, and Europe. Build alternative routes before retaliation arrives.

▶ Enact Data Localization Law
Legislation prohibiting the offshore transfer of domestic user data must be enacted without delay, establishing data localization as a legal baseline.

▶ Monitor Chinese Capital Influence
Civil society must continuously monitor and bring to public discourse the indirect influence of Chinese capital in media, politics, and academia.
