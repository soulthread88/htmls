⚡ 5 Ways to Protect Yourself From AI Voice Scams!
💡 Did You Know?
Over 100 million Americans have already tried an AI tool — and the easiest place to start is right here, right now. Let's go!
1 ① Hang Up and Call Back Directly
No matter how real the voice sounds — if someone calls from an unknown number asking for money, hang up IMMEDIATELY. Then YOU call your family member back at their real number. AI scammers cannot intercept that call. If the family member answers normally, it was a scam. Simple, effective, foolproof!
2 ② Create a Family Code Word RIGHT NOW
Tonight, create a secret word with your family that ONLY you all know — something an AI can never guess. Example: "What's Grandma's pie recipe secret ingredient?" If they can't answer, it's a scammer. This ONE step makes you 100% immune to AI voice cloning scams.
3 ③ The 30-Minute Rule — Never Send Money Immediately
Real emergencies can wait 30 minutes. Scams cannot — they pressure you to act within SECONDS. No matter what story you hear, wait 30 minutes, contact another family member, and confirm the situation independently before sending any money. Urgency is always the scammer's weapon!

The 4 AI Scam Methods Targeting American Seniors! Practical AI for your real, everyday American life
Jake: "Grandpa Bob, knowing exactly how these scams work is your best defense. I'm going to show you all four methods they use — and once you see them, you'll never be fooled by any of them!"
1. 📞 AI Voice Cloning — "It's Your Son Calling!"
Scammers collect 3 seconds of your family member's voice from social media or voicemail, then use AI to clone it perfectly — including accent, tone, and speech patterns. They call you crying "I've been arrested!" or "I had an accident!" The FBI reports this is now America's #1 growing elder scam!
2. 🤖 Government Impersonation Calls
An AI-generated voice claims to be from the IRS, Social Security Administration, or Medicare — saying your account is suspended or there's a warrant for your arrest. TRUTH: The IRS, SSA, and Medicare never call demanding immediate payment or threatening arrest. Hang up immediately — it's always a scam!
3. 💬 Deepfake Video Calls
Using AI, scammers create real-time video calls where a loved one's FACE is digitally applied to a scammer's body. Tip-offs: blurry or slightly unnatural edges around the face, mouth movements that don't perfectly sync with audio, or video that freezes or glitches when you ask them to turn sideways. Always call back separately!
4. 🏦 Text & Messaging Scams With Links
Scammers send texts pretending to be family or agencies, with links like "verify your account here." Clicking installs malware that steals your banking information and contacts. RULE: Never click any link in an unexpected text — no matter who it seems to be from. Call the person or organization directly instead!

🇺🇸 AI Vocabulary — Top 3 This Episode
1. Voice Cloning
"Cloning" means making an exact copy. "Voice cloning" uses AI to analyze a recording of someone's voice and recreate it with frightening accuracy — including their accent, emotion, and speech patterns. Only 3 seconds of audio is needed. This is the technology behind AI voice scams targeting American seniors.
2. Deepfake
"Deep" = deep learning AI. "Fake" = fabricated, not real. A "deepfake" is AI-generated audio or video that looks and sounds completely real but is entirely fabricated. Deepfake voice calls and video calls are becoming increasingly common scam tools. If something feels off, trust your gut and verify independently!
3. Social Engineering
"Social engineering" is the psychological manipulation of people into taking actions or revealing information. Scammers "engineer" your emotions — creating fear, urgency, and panic — to override your rational thinking. Recognizing this tactic is your best defense: when you feel rushed or panicked, STOP. That feeling is the scam working on you.
