KIND EXPLAINED

Easy to read,
never shallow.

We keep the Founding Issue's facts and figures intact, and add explanatory steps so readers without background knowledge can follow the logic all the way through.

1

30-second takeaway

The title and one-line summary give the page's conclusion first.

2

SOURCE FACT

Carries the founding issue’s facts; figures that conflict with primary sources are verified, corrected and tagged with a source code.

3

HOW · the mechanism

One diagram traces the flow from cause to effect.

4

In plain words

Explained in everyday language that needs no background knowledge.

5

Why is that?

Explains in a sentence or two why that flow happens.

6

KIND POINT

Leaves the reader one sentence to judge by.

7

Photos & video + sources

Free photos and video, credited with author, source and license.

!

Original facts and KIND's explanation are visually separated.

The gold-bordered SOURCE FACT is the original text; the explanation below it is KIND's. Unverified outside facts are never presented as original text, and each page carries only one core message.
