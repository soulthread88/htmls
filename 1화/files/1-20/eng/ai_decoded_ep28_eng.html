⚡ 3 Steps to Start AI Diet Tracking Today
💡 Did You Know?
Over 100 million Americans have already tried an AI tool — and the easiest place to start is right here, right now. Let's go!
1 Take a Photo of Your Meal
Open Calorie Mama or a similar AI food tracking app, tap the camera button, and photograph your plate. The AI identifies each food item and displays the calories, carbs, sodium, and key nutrients instantly — no typing!
2 Use the Color-Code System (Noom)
Noom classifies every food into green (low calorie, eat freely), yellow (moderate — eat mindfully), or red (high calorie — limit these). This simple traffic-light system makes healthy eating decisions effortless!
3 Ask ChatGPT for a Personalized Meal Plan
Type into ChatGPT: "Create a low-sodium, low-sugar weekly meal plan for a 70-year-old with diabetes." It will generate a detailed, customized plan in seconds — with breakfast, lunch, and dinner suggestions!

Saying Deep Dive Understand every word — and connect it to AI!
🔍 Breaking Down Today's Saying
"Let food be thy medicine and medicine be thy food."
Food (noun/verb/adj) (noun) any nutritious substance that living things eat or drink to maintain life and growth
Medicine (noun/verb/adj) (noun) a substance used for treating illness or injury; a remedy that promotes healing
Nourish (noun/verb/adj) (verb) to provide with substances needed for growth and good health; to feed and sustain
Heal (noun/verb/adj) (verb) to become healthy and whole again; to make someone recover from illness or injury
📌 Word-for-word meaning:
"Let food" (allow your food to be) "thy medicine" (your healing remedy) "and medicine be thy food" (the things that heal you should also be what you eat). "Thy" is an old English word for "your."
📌 In plain English:
Hippocrates, the father of modern medicine, said this over 2,400 years ago. He believed that the right food is the best medicine — and that the best medicine works like good food for the body.
📌 Real-life analogy:
Think of your body like a car engine. You wouldn't put low-quality fuel in a high-performance engine. Every meal you eat is fuel — AI diet apps help you choose premium fuel for your body, every single day.

🇺🇸 AI Vocabulary — Top 3 This Episode
1. Calorie
A unit of energy found in food. Your body needs a certain number of calories each day to function. AI diet apps tell you exactly how many calories are in every meal you photograph.
2. Sodium
The scientific name for salt. Too much sodium can raise your blood pressure and strain your heart. Daily recommended limit: about 2,000mg. AI diet apps track this for you automatically.
3. Glycemic Index (GI)
A number that shows how quickly a food raises your blood sugar. Low-GI foods (like vegetables and whole grains) are better for diabetics. ChatGPT can explain the GI of any food you ask about.
