Master Visual Identity — All 6 Shorts

Global Palette

#0A0A0A
#E8001A
#F5F5F5
#F59E0B
#1A56DB
#6EE7B7

Dominant: Near-black backgrounds. Accent: Signal red for danger/data. Amber for warnings. Mint for CTAs only.

Typography System

  • HOOK TEXT — Bebas Neue, 80–96px, white with 4px black stroke, ALL CAPS, center
  • Body captions — Barlow Condensed Bold, 52–58px, white, 3px black stroke
  • Stat callouts — DM Mono, 40px, amber #F59E0B, no stroke
  • Lower thirds — Barlow SemiBold, 28px, white on red bar
  • Max 4 words per caption frame. No sentences mid-screen.

Global Mood & Style

  • Aesthetic: Dark Investigative Editorial — 60 Minutes × WIRED × Vice News
  • No decorative elements. Functional. Urgent. Credible.
  • Grain overlay: 8% film noise on all background frames
  • Vignette: radial dark edge on all solid-color scenes
  • Motion: cuts every 2–4 sec. No slow fades mid-script.
  • Logo bug: IssueLens red dot + name, top-left, 32px, 60% opacity throughout

Format Specs (Vrew)

  • Aspect: 9:16 (1080 × 1920px)
  • Duration: 55–62 seconds each
  • FPS: 30fps
  • Caption font size in Vrew: Large / Bold
  • Caption position: Center-screen (60% height) — NOT bottom, avoids UI overlap
  • Caption color: White with Auto-black outline in Vrew

BGM Direction

  • Style: Low tension pulse bed — investigative documentary
  • Tempo: 70–85 BPM, minimal melody, sub-bass drone
  • VO volume: 100% / BGM: 12–15%
  • Beat hit: optional single percussive hit on the key stat reveal
  • Avoid: uplifting, motivational, lo-fi. Must feel like a news teaser.
  • Suggested free sources: Epidemic Sound "Investigation," YouTube Audio Library "Suspense"

Transition Rules

  • Hard cut: default between all scenes (0 frames)
  • Flash cut: 2-frame white flash on critical stat reveals
  • Zoom punch: 104% → 100% scale on hook word, 0.15s ease-out
  • Lower-third slide: 0.2s ease-in from left on agency names (FTC, FBI)
  • NO wipes, NO spins, NO glitter transitions
  • End card: hard cut to black → logo fade 0.4s → "Follow for more" 0.3s
