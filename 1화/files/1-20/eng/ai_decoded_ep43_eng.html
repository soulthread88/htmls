AMERICAN ENGLISH WORD POWER 3 essential AI terms — explained simply
📝
🇺🇸 AI VOCABULARY — TOP 3 THIS EPISODE
1. "GENERATIVE AI" (jen-uh-RAY-tiv A.I.)
A type of AI that creates brand-new content — songs, images, stories, code — rather than just answering questions. Suno AI is generative AI. It doesn't look up existing songs; it generates a completely new one just for you, in real time.
2. "PROMPT" (prahmt)
The instruction or description you type into an AI tool. Think of it like placing an order at a restaurant. The more specific your prompt — "country gospel ballad, acoustic guitar, emotional, about a father's love" — the better the song the AI serves you. Vague prompt = vague result.
3. "OUTPUT" (OWT-put)
What the AI produces in response to your prompt — in Suno AI's case, a finished song file you can download and share. The input is your story, the output is the song. Simple as that! When Grandpa Bob texts the family his song, that MP3 is the output.
